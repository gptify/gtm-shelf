# GTM Shelf brand kit (v1)

## Files
- svg/gtm-shelf-mark-*.svg      Mark only (cobalt, periwinkle for dark backgrounds, ink, white). Pure vector paths, transparent background.
- svg/gtm-shelf-icon.svg        App icon: white mark on a cobalt tile. icon-dark.svg is the dark-tile version.
- svg/gtm-shelf-favicon.svg     Favicon version, scaled up to stay readable at 16 px.
- svg/gtm-shelf-lockup-*.svg    Mark + wordmark, horizontal and stacked, light and dark. The wordmark is live text (see note below).
- png/                          Mark, icon, and favicon exports at common sizes.
- favicon.ico                   16, 32, and 48 px.

## Colors
- Cobalt      #2F45E0   primary, on light backgrounds
- Periwinkle  #8393FF   primary, on dark backgrounds
- Ink         #15172B   text and one-color use
- Mist        #F2F3F8   light background
- Night       #0F1122   dark background

## Type
- Wordmark: Bricolage Grotesque, tracking -0.025em, written "GTM Shelf" with two weights: GTM in ExtraBold (800), Shelf in Medium (500). Always capital G, T, M and a capital S.
- Body text on the site: Figtree.

## Use
- Clear space: keep at least half the height of the mark free on all sides.
- Minimum size: mark 16 px, lockup 96 px wide. Below that, use the favicon version.
- The two lighter blocks are the mark at 40 percent opacity. In one-color print, use the ink mark and keep the same opacity or a 40 percent tint.
- Always keep GTM heavier than Shelf. Do not set the wordmark in one weight or all lowercase.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor outside the palette, or place the cobalt mark on a busy photo.

## Note on the wordmark
The lockup SVGs use live text set in Bricolage Grotesque. Where that font is not installed they fall back to a system font. Before print or partner use, open the lockup in a design tool with the font installed and convert the text to outlines.
