# Legal drafts

**These are drafts, not legal advice.** They were written from general knowledge, without knowing the legal entity, its country, or where visitors will be. A qualified lawyer in the relevant jurisdiction must review and adapt them before launch. Anything in [BRACKETS] must be filled in or removed.

Points a lawyer should check in particular:
- Which law applies (visitors in the EU, UK, US, and Uzbekistan can each bring different rules).
- The correct wording of the imprint for the country of operation. Germany, for example, requires an imprint (Impressum) with specific fields.
- Rules on marketing emails, double opt-in, and unsubscribe in each target country.
- Rules on labelling paid placements and affiliate links.
- Whether a data processing agreement is needed with each service provider.
- Whether the data protection contact or representative is required.
