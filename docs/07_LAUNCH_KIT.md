# Launch kit

Ready-to-edit copy and a timeline. Replace [BRACKETS]. Only claim numbers that are true on the day (tool count, verified date).

**Audience assets to use:** the English GPTify LinkedIn page (about 34,000 followers) and its newsletter (about 15,000 subscribers), plus the personal LinkedIn profile. Engagement on the page has been low, so lead with something people can interact with (the finder) instead of announcements.

**Principles**
- Give value first: a tool answer, not an ad.
- Be honest about what the site is and that GPTify runs it.
- Never post the same text twice in the same community.
- No fake reviews, no fake upvotes, no asking friends to vote in bulk.

## 7. Launch timeline

**T minus 14 days**
- Domain, email, and analytics working. Legal pages reviewed.
- 100 or more tools verified, at least 6 per category.
- Vendor "your tool is listed" emails sent.

**T minus 7 days**
- Full QA pass (`docs/08_QA_CHECKLIST.md`).
- Screenshots and Product Hunt assets ready.
- Newsletter and post drafts finished and scheduled.

**Launch day**
- Publish the site. Post on LinkedIn (personal, then the company page), send the newsletter, launch on Product Hunt.
- Answer every comment and message within a few hours.

**T plus 1 to 7 days**
- Post 2 and Post 3. Post in one or two communities.
- Fix every wrong tool detail that was reported, the same day.
- Read finder runs and see where people drop off.

**T plus 14 days**
- Post 4 with real data. Review analytics against the goals below.

## 8. Goals and metrics (adjust to your own targets)

- Finder completion rate (completed divided by started).
- Share of finder results with a click on Visit or Details.
- Custom build requests per week, and how many are qualified.
- Vendor submissions and vendor replies.
- Indexed pages and first impressions in Search Console.
- Newsletter confirmation rate (confirmed divided by requested).
